Admin Core

The work gets done. You still decide.

Admin Core is one coordinated set of agents and one local model, working inside the systems your business already runs. Your colleague prepares the work, drafts the replies and keeps the record. Anything that matters stops at a named person, and that decision is logged against them.

Your people stay in charge.

No colleague holds the send button. Every action that carries consequence is prepared below and approved by a person above, and the trail shows who decided what and when.

Routine work runs on its own.

The post is read, sorted and answered. Policy questions are answered from your own documents, with the document named. The daily report tells you what happened and what needs you.

It runs on your terms.

Managed by us, or on your own hardware. Your data stays inside the boundary you choose, and we will tell you plainly which one you are buying.
