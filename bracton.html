Bracton · Licence and implementation support

Keep your AI’s rules when the model changes.

Bracton is our deterministic safety layer around AI models. We license it on its own to organisations that build or run their own AI, and we help you fit it, so your safeguards hold whichever model does the work.

Talk to us about a licence

WHAT IT DOES

Bracton puts seven protections round the model: guarded inputs, no direct power, checked outputs, written limits, people decide, a full record and safe by default. Each one is ordinary code outside the model, so none of them relies on the model behaving.

WHAT YOU GET

You get a licence to run Bracton round your own AI, on infrastructure you control. We map the work with you, write down what the AI may do on its own and what waits for a person, and fit Bracton round it. We test it on your own work before anything goes live, and we stay on hand when you change model.

Who it’s for

It suits UK SMEs and regulated organisations that build or run their own AI, and the software teams that build AI for them.

On its own or with Admin Core

Bracton stands alone. Admin Core, our AI automation platform for back office work, runs inside it and is live with clients.
