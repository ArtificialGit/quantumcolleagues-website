Agency-of-one (productised service)

A productised service is expertise turned into a package: one audience, one outcome, one price, delivered the same way every time. Instead of selling your hours, you sell a result. This suits people who like craft and repetition, who would rather perfect one service than reinvent every job. The honest hard parts are choosing narrowly enough, the discipline to say no to bespoke work, and quiet patches early on. It is newly possible because AI can run the production and the back office, so one person can deliver at a pace that used to need an agency floor.

A day in this job

Two client jobs move through the pipeline today, each following the same steps as always. AI produced the raw drafts overnight, so your morning is spent lifting them to your standard. After lunch there is a discovery call with a prospect, then you queue the next job. Invoices and reminders sent themselves.

How AI is changing it

AI runs the pipeline: drafting, producing, scheduling, invoicing, chasing. Because every job follows the same process, it automates beautifully. What stays human is the standard, the judgement on every piece before it ships, the client relationships, and the promise you make and keep.

What you need to start

There is no entry gate. What matters is one packaged offer with one price, a repeatable delivery process, and first clients drawn from people who already trust your work.

The steps, in order

  1. Productise one offer Package your expertise into one named service: one audience, one outcome, one price, delivered the same way every time.
  2. Build the AI pipeline Create a repeatable AI-assisted process for delivery, so every job follows the same steps to the same standard.
  3. Sort the money basics Set up contracts, deposits, invoicing and a tax pot before the first client, not after the first problem.
  4. Land anchor clients Start with people who already trust your work. Deliver brilliantly, then ask each one for a referral.
  5. Show your proof Turn finished jobs into short case stories with real results, so strangers can see what buying from you gets.
  6. Systemise and scale Let AI run the repeatable parts of delivery while you sell, and add a funded business bootcamp if useful.
