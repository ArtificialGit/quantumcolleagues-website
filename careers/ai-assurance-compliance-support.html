AI assurance / compliance support

AI assurance work means checking that AI systems are safe, fair and accountable, before and after they are used on real people. It is one of the newest careers around, growing as the rules tighten, and because it is so new, careful outsiders can get in early. It suits sceptical, methodical people who prefer evidence to hype: you test claims, document findings and keep honest records. The hard parts are the pace of change and the amount of reading. The purpose is easy to believe in.

A day in this job

You spend the morning running checks on an AI system: does it treat different groups consistently, does it fail safely, are its records complete. You write up what you found, good and bad. In the afternoon you meet the team who built it and agree the fixes. You end by updating the audit trail.

How AI is changing it

AI can generate the test cases and the paperwork for its own inspection, which speeds up the routine work enormously. But a system cannot credibly vouch for itself. Deciding whether it is fair, safe and fit to use, and putting your name to that, stays human.

What you need to start

Entrants normally have a degree, GCSEs, a BTEC or A levels. Training is mostly given on the job.

Typical pay: £680 a week (about £35,400 a year).

The steps, in order

  1. Learn what assurance covers Free courses on safe, fair AI explain what checking a system involves: bias, safety, record keeping and accountability.
  2. Practise checking real AI Check AI output regularly and keep a log of what you catch. Documented findings become your first portfolio.
  3. Learn the testing discipline Free introductions to software testing teach the methodical habits assurance borrows: plan the checks, run them, record everything.
  4. Find a funded route in Funded Skills Bootcamps in data and digital skills give eligible adults a structured, no cost way into the field.
  5. Add a recognised credential A BCS qualification signals you take quality seriously, which helps in a field too new for standard job titles.
  6. Apply, then help set standards AI assurance support roles are appearing now. Once in, grow by writing the checklists that others will follow.
