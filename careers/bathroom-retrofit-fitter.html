Bathroom / retrofit fitter

Bathroom and retrofit fitters turn tired rooms and draughty houses into finished, working spaces, combining plumbing, tiling, joinery and basic electrics. It suits people who like seeing a job through from strip-out to final silicone bead, and who take pride in the before-and-after photo. Honest downsides: dust, heavy materials up narrow stairs, and customers living around your work. Renovation demand stays steady, and the national push to retrofit older homes for energy efficiency is adding a whole new stream of funded work.

A day in this job

You arrive to a room that is half building site and leave it a little closer to finished. One day is ripping out and first fix, another is tiling, and the best day is fitting the gleaming final pieces. You sweep up properly, because the homeowner is living ten feet away.

How AI is changing it

AI speeds up quoting from photos, ordering materials, and keeping several jobs scheduled without double-booking. Design tools help customers see the finished room before you start. The craft, the problem solving when the walls are not square, and the trust of working in someone's home stay yours.

What you need to start

There are no formal entry requirements, though GCSEs help. People typically train through an apprenticeship or college course with on-the-job learning, or build up experience with short courses.

Typical pay: £670 a week (about £34,800 a year).

The steps, in order

  1. Explore fitting work Read bathroom fitting and retrofit profiles to see what the work involves and what it pays.
  2. Learn the trades involved Fitting mixes plumbing, tiling, joinery and basic electrics, so build each skill through short courses.
  3. Use the funded routes Adults without a Level 3 qualification can often train free, and apprenticeships are funded at any age.
  4. Build a portfolio Photograph every job from strip-out to finish, because proof of clean work wins referrals.
  5. Go solo or join a firm Steady renovation demand from homeowners and landlords means good fitters rarely wait long for work.
  6. Grow once you're in Add retrofit skills like insulation and ventilation, where funded courses and long-term demand are strongest.
