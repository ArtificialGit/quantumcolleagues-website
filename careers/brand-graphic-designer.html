Brand / graphic designer

Graphic and brand designers give organisations a face: logos, layouts, packaging and the look of everything a customer touches. The tools have never been cheaper, which means the value has moved to judgement, knowing what fits, what is honest and what will still look right in five years. It suits visual people who like solving real problems, not just decorating. The hard parts are real: freelancing swings between feast and famine, clients can be vague, and AI now floods the market with average visuals. Designers who can think, explain their choices and direct the tools are the ones clients keep.

A day in this job

The morning might go to concepts for a local firm's rebrand, sketching options and testing them against the brief. Midday you present two directions on a call and note the reaction. The afternoon is production: tidying files, preparing a menu for print, chasing feedback. You end by updating your portfolio.

How AI is changing it

AI generates images, layouts and endless variations in seconds, and clients know it. Routine production work is shrinking. What stays human is art direction: taste, judgement about what fits the brand and its audience, and the relationship where a client trusts your eye over the machine's output.

What you need to start

Most entrants have completed a foundation course, a BTEC, a degree or similar, and design qualifications exist at Levels 2 and 3. A portfolio of real work matters just as much.

Typical pay: £700 a week (about £36,400 a year).

The steps, in order

  1. Learn the basics Layout, type and colour are the core. Free short courses cover them.
  2. Use the AI design tools Use AI image and layout tools to speed things up. Keep the art direction yours.
  3. Design for three real things Rebrand a local club, a market stall or a charity event. Real briefs beat invented ones.
  4. Build a portfolio that shows judgement Show the thinking, not just the pretty final frame.
  5. Show, then sell A simple online portfolio plus direct approaches to small businesses gets first work.
  6. Grow into brand direction The senior work is deciding what a brand should be. AI can't do that for you.
