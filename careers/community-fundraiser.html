Community fundraiser

Community fundraisers raise the money that keeps charities alive, through events, appeals, local businesses and loyal supporters. It suits sociable, resilient people who can ask for help without embarrassment and keep going after a no. The honest bits: targets follow you around, evenings and weekends happen, and early-career pay is ordinary. It is well worth considering now because people who can ask well are permanently scarce, and every charity in the country needs the money they raise.

A day in this job

You might spend the morning calling supporters and thanking donors from the weekend's event. Midday could be coffee with a local business owner who wants to help. Afternoons go on planning the next appeal, updating the supporter database and drafting posts. Event weeks flip all of this on its head.

How AI is changing it

AI now drafts appeals, donor emails, event plans and campaign reports, and reads the data for you. The asking is not automated. What stays human is the relationship: telling a true story well, earning a donor's trust and knowing your own community.

What you need to start

There are no formal entry requirements, though many people come in with A levels, a BTEC or a degree. Training is usually in-house, with short courses and professional qualifications on top.

Typical pay: £730 a week (about £38,000 a year).
Official UK pay for "Advertising and marketing associate professionals" (ONS ASHE 2022, via LMI for All). A guide, not a promise.

The steps, in order

  1. Volunteer on a campaign Join a local fundraising event or appeal and learn how it works from inside.
  2. Learn the craft Free short courses cover the fundraising basics. The fundraisers' professional body publishes good guides.
  3. Bank small wins Run a community raffle, a sponsored event or a grant application. Each one is proof.
  4. Learn the data side Fundraisers who can read supporter records and campaign reports stand out.
  5. Apply for jobs Fundraising assistant and community fundraiser jobs value exactly this kind of proof.
  6. Grow into major giving The senior work is built on relationships. That is where AI helps least and you matter most.
