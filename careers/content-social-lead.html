Content & social lead

Every organisation now publishes daily, and someone has to decide what it says. Content and social leads run a brand's voice across channels: planning, writing, posting and reading the numbers. It suits people who are curious about audiences, quick with words and calm when a post lands badly. The trade-offs are real: the feed never sleeps, results are public, and trends move fast. Demand is broad and steady though, from charities to builders' merchants, and it is one of the friendliest doors into marketing. You can prove yourself with a real account before anyone hires you.

A day in this job

You start by checking how yesterday's posts performed and what people are saying. Then you plan the week's content, write and schedule a few posts, and brief a designer or film something quick on your phone. Afternoons might bring a campaign meeting and replies to comments. You end by noting what worked.

How AI is changing it

AI now drafts posts, captions and content calendars, and summarises the analytics in plain English. That speeds up the production side enormously. What stays human is the judgement: knowing the brand's voice, sensing what the audience actually cares about, and holding the standard for what goes out.

What you need to start

No formal qualifications are required, though many people come in with A levels, a BTEC or a degree, and most training happens on the job. A portfolio of real results opens more doors than certificates.

Typical pay: £730 a week (about £38,000 a year).

The steps, in order

  1. Learn the channels See how content works on each platform and what makes people stop scrolling. Free short courses cover the basics.
  2. Run a real account Grow your own channel or offer to run one for a local charity. Real numbers become your proof.
  3. Explore funded courses Skills Bootcamps sometimes cover digital marketing and content skills, free for many adults. Check what runs in your area.
  4. Build a results portfolio Show what grew and why it grew. Screenshots of real numbers beat a wall of pretty posts.
  5. Apply or freelance Small firms, charities and agencies all need content leads. Pitch with your results, not just your writing.
  6. Grow into strategy Once you're in, aim at planning and brand voice. Deciding what the brand says pays more than posting it.
