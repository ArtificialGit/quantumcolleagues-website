Fractional operations manager

Fractional means part-time by design: you run operations for several small firms at once, each paying for a slice of your week. For the firms, it is the operations leader they could never afford full-time. For you, it is variety, independence and income from more than one source. It suits experienced organisers who like ownership without a single employer. The honest hard parts are juggling loyalties, uneven months while the client list builds, and being your own back office. AI is what makes the maths work: it carries the routine load across every client, so your hours go on judgement.

A day in this job

Monday might belong to one client and Tuesday to another. Each morning AI hands you a brief per client: what ran, what stuck, what needs you. Your hours go on decisions, people and the odd fire, not on compiling anything. On Friday afternoon the invoices go out on their own.

How AI is changing it

AI carries the routine work across every client: reports, schedules, reminders, invoices. That is what lets one person serve several firms well. What stays human is the judgement on what each business needs, the relationships with each owner, and the promises you make and keep.

What you need to start

There is no gate. What matters is one firm whose operations you demonstrably fixed, a clearly packaged offer, and the confidence to pitch the time and money you save.

The steps, in order

  1. Package your skill Write down exactly what you will run for a small firm: the systems, the reporting and the results they get.
  2. Prove it once Fix one firm's operations end to end, measure the hours and money saved, and write the story up.
  3. Add a credential A BCS or digital qualification gives your skills external weight when a stranger is deciding whether to trust you.
  4. Get the money side right Set up simple contracts, monthly invoicing and a cash buffer, because several small clients means several small payment dates.
  5. Win your first clients Pitch the time and money you saved, with real numbers, to firms too small to hire a full-time manager.
  6. Grow the portfolio Add clients one at a time, keep the service consistent, and let AI carry the routine work across all of them.
