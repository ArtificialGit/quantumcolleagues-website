Motion & social designer

Motion and social designers make the short videos, animations and graphics that fill every feed. Demand is constant because attention is scarce: every brand, venue and charity needs fresh content weekly, not yearly. It suits designers with rhythm, people who feel timing the way musicians do and enjoy quick turnarounds over long projects. The trade-offs are real: the pace is relentless, trends date fast, and freelance income can lurch between busy and quiet months. The tools are cheap though, AI removes much of the grunt work, and a maker who posts consistently can build a client base from a bedroom.

A day in this job

You start by checking which of yesterday's posts moved and why. The morning goes to animating a product promo for one client, the afternoon to cutting three short clips for another. Between renders you draft captions, answer messages and queue tomorrow's work. You post one piece of your own before you finish.

How AI is changing it

AI now generates footage, animates stills, cuts rough edits and writes captions, so volume is no longer the hard part. What stays human is the idea, the timing and the taste that make one clip land while a thousand look the same, plus the client who trusts you to deliver.

What you need to start

Entrants usually come through a foundation course, a BTEC, a degree or similar, with design qualifications at Levels 2 and 3 also available. A reel of real work counts for more than any of them.

Typical pay: £700 a week (about £36,400 a year).

The steps, in order

  1. Master the tools Free tutorials cover motion basics, editing and social formats. A phone and free software are enough to start.
  2. Use AI to speed output Let AI generate assets, variations and rough cuts while you keep the art direction and the taste.
  3. Publish weekly A regular reel builds your eye, your timing and an audience at the same time. Consistency beats polish.
  4. Get funded support If you're 16 to 30, the King's Trust offers free help to start earning from your work.
  5. Charge small brands Local brands need motion content every month. Start with two or three clients and let referrals build.
  6. Grow into direction Move from single posts to running whole campaigns. Direction pays better than production, and AI can't do it.
