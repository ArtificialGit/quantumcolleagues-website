Plumber

Plumbers install and repair the water, waste and heating systems every home and business depends on, which is why the work never dries up. It suits people who like variety, independence and fixing real problems for real people. Honestly, it can be cramped, wet and heavy, with plenty of time on your knees under sinks and floors. The move from gas boilers to low-carbon heating means plumbing and heating skills are in strong demand, and hands willing to retrain towards heat pumps will stay busy for years.

A day in this job

The day might start with a leaking valve in one house and end fitting a bathroom in another. You cut and join pipe, test for leaks, and tidy as you go, because you are working in someone's home. There is driving between jobs, a merchant run for parts, and a phone that rarely stays quiet.

How AI is changing it

AI is taking the drag out of the office side: quotes, invoices, scheduling and parts lookup happen in minutes. Photo-based diagnosis can help you arrive with the right part first time. The fix itself, the judgement under the floorboards and the trust of being welcomed into a home stay human.

What you need to start

There are no formal entry requirements, though GCSEs help. Most plumbers train through an apprenticeship or a college course combined with on-the-job training, or build up experience with short courses.

Typical pay: £670 a week (about £34,800 a year).
Official UK pay for "Plumbers and heating and ventilating installers and repairers" (ONS ASHE 2022, via LMI for All). A guide, not a promise.

The steps, in order

  1. Explore plumbing and heating Read about plumber and heating engineer jobs to see what the work is like.
  2. Take a taster or evening course A short intro course at a local college lets you test it before committing.
  3. Train via apprenticeship or diploma Both routes are funded and lead to the same place.
  4. Get gas-safe and qualified The recognised qualifications open the regulated, better-paid work.
  5. Specialise in low-carbon heating Heat pumps and retrofit are where the growth is. Get there early.
  6. Build a steady customer base Reliable heating engineers are never short of work.
