The philosophy

Why we introduce through a network, not an ad budget

SMEs, charities and social enterprises buy our AI products, and a share of every pound we earn funds free post-AI training and career planning for every generation. That model only works if the way we grow is as honest as the mission. So we choose people over reach. Our products are introduced by people who know the business, understand the mission, and put their own name next to ours when they open a door.

An introduction is a different thing from an advert. An advert interrupts you. An introduction comes from someone who thought of you specifically, and who answers for the recommendation afterwards. We think owners of small organisations deserve the second kind, and we have built our whole approach around it.

Our associates are self-employed and openly commission based. We say that plainly because it is a feature, not a secret. Nobody in our network earns anything unless a client chooses us, stays with us, and pays for something that genuinely works. That puts every incentive on the right side of the table: our people do well only when our clients do.

The rules

What every person introducing us signs up to

Support, not substitution

Our AI gives a human team extra capacity, and we don’t replace people.

No pressure, ever

If we genuinely can’t help a client, we won’t pretend we can or try to.

Compliance matters

Our customers’ compliance is as important to us as our own.

The company answers for everything

Associates open doors; price, delivery and contract terms are agreed only with the company itself, so what the customer is promised is what they get.

Why it matters. Our mission is to maximise our ability to fund future training and career planning, for every generation. That promise is written into our constitution, not our marketing, and it walks into every room our network opens.
