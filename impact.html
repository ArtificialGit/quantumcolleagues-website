Most companies ask you to take it on trust. We’d rather show you. QuantumColleagues is a Community Interest Company: when a business buys AI from us, a defined share is locked to a social mission — funding post-AI training for the people the market usually skips. This page is the receipt: the mechanism, the numbers, and where to verify every claim on it.

How it works

One purchase. Two jobs.

Step 1
You buy AI

A business buys Proust, Admin Core, or a project crew — the same tools you’d pay for anyway.

Step 2
We lock the value

We’re a CIC. Profits are asset-locked and dividends capped by law. The good can’t be quietly extracted — mine included.

Step 3
It funds two

A defined share funds post-AI training for two people who’d never otherwise get it: a young person starting out, and someone later in life.

The promises behind the numbers

What we’ve bound ourselves to.

  • Asset-locked by law. The value we build stays bound to the mission. It can’t be sold off or paid out to enrich owners.
  • Dividends capped. A CIC can pay out no more than 35% of profit. The rest stays in the mission.
  • Profit reinvested first. The majority of profit goes back into training. Dividends are the exception, not the point.
  • Reported every year. Our community impact is filed publicly in the CIC34 report at Companies House.
