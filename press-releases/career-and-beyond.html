QuantumColleagues launches Career & Beyond — a free career change tool for every generation

The UK AI-for-good company releases its first free give back: a web app that maps working life as a tree, starting from the skills people already have. No sign up, no cost, on any phone.

For immediate release  ·  21 July 2026  ·  Northamptonshire, UK

AI is reshaping the jobs millions of people do today. Retail, admin, driving, call centre work and bookkeeping are all being redrawn, yet most careers advice still assumes a ladder: climb it or fall behind. QuantumColleagues™, the Northamptonshire AI company built to give back, has launched a free tool that assumes a tree instead.

Career & Beyond maps working life as seven growing branches and starts from the skills a person already has. Someone who reads a room well in retail sees how that same skill anchors care and support work. Someone who runs a back office sees how, with AI handling the routine, they could run one for a whole business, or their own. Each move comes with real jobs, real pay and real first steps that can be taken this week. One branch, Beyond, covers life outside work altogether, because a life is bigger than a job.

The tool is free for every generation, from fifteen to eighty five. There is no sign up, no account and no data collected. It works on any phone and keeps working offline.

Career & Beyond is the company’s first free give back, funded by its commercial work. Businesses buy QuantumColleagues’ AI products, and that revenue funds post-AI training for everyone — a commitment protected by an asset-locked Community Interest Company that owns half the trading business.

“Career & Beyond is the first thing we have shipped that costs the person using it nothing. That matters to us. We sell AI to businesses precisely so we can build things like this. Your life was never a ladder. It’s a tree, and it’s ready to grow.”

— Jonathan Clark, Founder, QuantumColleagues™

Career & Beyond is available now at quantumcolleagues.org/career-beyond.
