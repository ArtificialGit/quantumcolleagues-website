Privacy Policy

Effective 25 July 2026

Questions about this policy or your data: support@quantumcolleagues.org

About Proust

Proust (“Proust”, “the Service”, “we”, “us”) is an AI executive-assistant service for founders. It reads the inbox and calendar you connect, prepares email drafts for your approval, and sends you a short daily brief. Proust prepares work for you. It does not send email to other people as you.

Proust is provided by QuantumColleagues Ltd, registered in England and Wales, company number 17219359. This policy explains what data Proust accesses from your Google Account, how that data is used, and the choices available to you.

The Google data Proust accesses, and why

When you connect a Google Account, Proust requests only the access it needs to run the Service:

  • Gmail — read and manage (gmail.modify): Proust reads incoming mail to triage and prioritise it, prepares draft replies in your Drafts folder for you to approve, and marks mail it has handled as read. This scope does not permit permanent deletion of your mail.
  • Calendar — read only (calendar.readonly): Proust reads your calendar to flag clashes, prepare you ahead of meetings, and include your schedule in your daily brief. Proust does not create or change calendar events.
  • Basic account information (userinfo.email, userinfo.profile): your email address and basic profile, such as your name, identify your account and personalise your briefings.

Proust does not send email through your Gmail account. When Proust sends its own messages, such as your welcome note or daily brief, it sends them from its own QuantumColleagues mailbox, not from you.

How we use your information

We use this information solely to provide and operate Proust for you: triaging and prioritising your mail, preparing drafts for your approval, reading your diary for context, and producing your daily brief. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not use it to train generalised or third-party AI models.

Limited Use (Google API Services User Data Policy)

Proust’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

In particular: we use Google user data only to provide and improve the Proust features you have enabled; we do not sell this data; we do not use it for advertising; we do not transfer it to others except as needed to provide the Service, for security or legal reasons, or in connection with a merger or acquisition with appropriate notice; and we do not allow humans to read your data except where you have given consent, where it is necessary for security or to comply with applicable law, or where the data has been aggregated and anonymised.

How Proust processes and protects your data

  • The AI runs on our own infrastructure. Proust’s language model is self-hosted on QuantumColleagues infrastructure. The content of your mail is never sent to any third-party AI service.
  • Encrypted at rest. Data Proust retains is encrypted at rest.
  • Your own instance. Each founder’s Proust runs in its own isolated instance, with its own storage. Your data is not mixed with another founder’s.
  • No advertising or profiling. We run no advertising and build no profiles for sale.

Who else touches your data

We keep the list of third parties short, and use them only to deliver the Service:

  • Google — your own account, which you grant Proust access to, and can revoke at any time.
  • Microsoft 365 — Proust sends its own outbound mail (its welcome and brief, and any reply you approve) from its QuantumColleagues mailbox, which is hosted on Microsoft 365. The content of those messages passes through Microsoft to be delivered.
  • Todoist — only if you choose to connect it, and only to add the tasks you ask Proust to add.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with anyone else, except where we are required to by law or to protect the security of the Service.

How long we keep it

We keep your data only as long as needed to run Proust for you. When you stop using Proust or revoke access, we delete the data Proust holds within 30 days.

Revoking access

You are in control. You can disconnect Proust at any time from your Google Account security settings at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and you can ask us to delete your data by writing to the address below. Revoking access stops Proust reading your account immediately.

Your rights

If you are in the UK or the EU, you have rights under UK GDPR and the GDPR, including the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict its processing. To exercise any of these, contact us at the address below. The data controller is QuantumColleagues Ltd (company number 17219359).

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Proust develops. When we make a material change, we will update the effective date above and, where appropriate, tell you directly.
