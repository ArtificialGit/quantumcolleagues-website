Proust · Founder Assist

Not a mail app.
A proper assistant.

Proust is not a plugin bolted onto your inbox. It is an assistant of its own, with its own name and its own mailbox. You connect it to your Google or Microsoft account, and to Todoist, the way you would hand a new assistant a login. Then it gets on with the work between your check-ins. It minds your inbox, sorts your diary and briefs you each morning on what actually needs you. Quietly, without being asked. Its own emails, like your welcome note and your daily brief, come from Proust, and never from you.

WHAT PROUST DOES

Five jobs, off your plate, from day one.

■Reads your inbox

It triages, drafts and files. Each morning you get the three things that truly need you, not the ninety that do not.

■Runs your diary

It books, moves and chases meetings, and it keeps the context when something has to shift.

■Keeps your list moving

Your Todoist stays in step with your inbox and your diary, so nothing you meant to do quietly slips.

■Briefs you every morning

Every day at 8, a sixty second read with your first coffee. What is urgent, what is on, who needs you.

■Remembers everything

Names, decisions, drafts, the thing you asked for last Tuesday. It never asks you the same question twice.

YOUR DATA, YOUR CALL

You are always in charge. Proust prepares work for you to approve. It does not send email to other people as you, and it cannot delete your mail. You can see everything it has access to, and take that access away, at any time. On the Managed plan your email is handled in QuantumColleagues’ own private environment in the UK or EU. On the Appliance, Proust runs on your own hardware and your data never leaves your site. Either way, it is auditable by design. The full detail sits in the privacy policy.
