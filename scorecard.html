FREE SCORECARD · FOR OWNERS AND MANAGERS

Safe AI Readiness Scorecard

ten questions, three minutes, a plain result

Your people are already using AI. This scorecard tells you whether anyone is in control of it. It scores three things: where your data goes, who decides, and whether anyone keeps the record. The result shows on this page. Nothing leaves your browser unless you ask us for the briefing at the end.

Data · where it goes

1. Do people in your organisation use public AI tools (ChatGPT, Copilot, Gemini and the like) for work?
2. Has client, resident, customer or staff information ever been pasted into a public AI tool?
3. If a regulator, an insurer or a client asked where your data goes when AI is used, could you answer in writing today?
4. Who owns the data and the record of what was asked and answered?

Control · who decides

5. When AI drafts something that matters (a letter to a client, a complaint response, a contract, a report), who decides whether it goes out?
6. Is there a written line between what AI may do on its own and what it may only prepare for a person?
7. Can you show an auditor or inspector a record of what AI did last week in your organisation?

People · who knows the rules

8. Do your staff know what they may and may not put into an AI tool?
9. Who is accountable, by name, for AI use in your organisation?
10. Do you measure the time AI is meant to save you?

    How it is scored

    Three pillars, each out of ten. Three tiers. No percentage we have not measured in your own organisation.

    ■Exposed, 0 to 10

    AI is in use and nobody controls it. The data is already outside your perimeter. Two handbook rules go in this week.

    ■Managed, 11 to 20

    You have rules, and gaps. The line is not drawn or not signed, or the record is partial. Draw the line and name the four people.

    ■Ready, 21 to 30

    The controls exist. The question is whether the tools match them. You are ready for a colleague inside your own systems.
