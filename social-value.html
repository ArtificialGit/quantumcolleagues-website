CSR and social value

AI that does some good, on the record.

For UK partners, service providers and organisations that report CSR and social value.

Most AI spend leaves nothing behind for the community. Ours does. Every Admin Core deployment funds free post-AI training and career planning for young people and people in later life, through Quantum Colleagues Training CIC. The organisation whose purchase funded it receives an impact report in its own name, as evidence for its CSR, ESG and social value reporting.

How it works

One purchase. Two jobs.

Step 1
An organisation buys Admin Core

It buys directly, through a partner, or inside a service that a provider already runs for it. Admin Core is the AI back office crew that is live with clients today.

Step 2
The CIC funds the training

Quantum Colleagues Training CIC owns half of QuantumColleagues Ltd under an asset lock, so its share of the profit stays with the mission.

Step 3
One report, one owner

The CIC reports each deployment's impact once, to the organisation whose purchase funded it, so nothing is counted twice.

What you can report

Evidence, not adjectives.

  • Free post-AI training, funded by the purchase. For young people starting out and people in later life.
  • Delivered near your sites, or nationally. The training reaches the communities you serve.
  • An impact statement in your own name. Ready for CSR, ESG and social value reports.
  • Checkable on the public record. The CIC files a yearly community report at Companies House, and its asset lock sits in its constitution.

Public sector buyers often ask for social value in bids. The pack gives your answers real evidence; how a bid is scored is always the buyer's decision.
