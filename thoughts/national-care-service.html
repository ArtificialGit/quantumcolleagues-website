A National Care Service is coming. The admin is already here.

Social care · 29 September 2026

The Prime Minister has promised a National Care Service, free at the point of delivery. Working out the organisation and the funding will take a while. In the meantime the whole sector is focused on getting more of the money it already has onto actual care.

A very large share of a care provider’s week goes on compliance registers, training renewals, contract dates and correspondence rather than on the people it looks after.

We already have Admin Core, our back office agent crew, working today inside regulated UK organisations. It reads and sorts the post, keeps the registers, chases the renewals and drafts the letters. Anything with real world consequence is governed by our threshold model, every human decision is logged, and all of it is designed to support what the CQC expects.
